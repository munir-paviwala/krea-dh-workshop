Critiquing DH projects (Part 1) +
1. Bollywood and Beyond: Analyzing Indian Film Industry through Irfan's Guftagoo Interviews

UPenn project of analysis of film industry demographics. Focus on data visualisations.

2. Climate Change | Our world in Data

Our world in data is an excellent source. Focus on how data and argument work together.

3. Neponset River Lab Project

GIS project that is more public facing. Focus on entries.

4. Building blocks of Brahminical Patriarchy

One of the few basic EPW interactive journalism piece. (Also see another no.4)

4. NYT Notre Dame Cathedral Acoustics and Sound

NYT interactive from 2023. Allows superb immersion.

5. Past India Collection

A blog with miscellaneous but interesting collections. Focus on how the collection is organised.

6. Bichitra :: Online Tagore Vaiorum

Jadavpur university project creating a digital archive focused on Tagore. Focus on how the collection is organised.

7. Tasveer Ghar project (Focus on Essays!)

Amazing project collecting images from South Asia with good essays.

8. The 1947 Partition Archive

A Delhi University project. Check the oral history collection.

9. Photogrammar

A collection of photos from US through 19th and 20th century.

10. Overview: 103 Orchard

History of an apartment in NYC. Focus on use of multimedia sources to create immersion for the argument.
